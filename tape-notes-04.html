Tape Notes · 04

1 of 1 is not an edge. A week of green is not a regime.

Win rate is a headline. Sample size is the story. This issue is how to read a public scoreboard without lying to yourself — and without adding size to celebrate.

Job: keep the book honest when the chip is 100% and when it is ugly. Education only.

01 · The chip that lies

Example · not a live week

100% on one ticket

1 / 1100%
2 / 729%
4 / 1233%
Color of one chip ≠ system.

Math is true. Meaning is not. One winner is a path. Last week can still be 1–6. Both can sit on the same page.

  • Always read "1 of 1 today," not "100% system."
  • Contract % is not account %. +20% on $50 is not +20% on the book.
  • One hero trade can hide a pile of small reds. Ask for net dollars.
Yield: if removing one ticket flips the month, you do not have an edge. You have a concentration.

02 · What a sample even is

Fills, not calendar days

You haveYou can say
1–5 fillsMechanics work or don't. Not expectancy.
~15–30Hints. Still one cluster away from a different story.
50+Start talking about a rate. Still not a license to size up blindly.

Quiet days with zero fills do not count as wins. They count as process if passing was the rule.

03 · Streaks

Six reds in a row is allowed

A 30% win-rate book will print ugly strings. That does not mean the next ticket must be forced so the board looks better.

  • Streaks feel like information. Usually they are clustering.
  • Changing the rule mid-streak is how you lock in the old mistake and add a new one.
  • A green snap-back after six reds is not proof the snap-back method works.
Yield: pre-commit what a bad week looks like (dollar cap, fill cap) so the streak cannot write policy.

04 · Expectancy beats win rate

The adult column

Average win, average loss, how often each happens. A 25% win rate with 3:1 payoff can live. A 60% win rate with tiny wins and fat disaster tails cannot.

  • Watch disaster-floor hits separately from ordinary cuts.
  • Time-cuts that stay small are different from −50% quote blowups.
  • If disasters are common, the product or the entry is wrong — not the "mindset."

05 · When to change size

Not after Wednesday

Size steps up when process is boring and the sample is real. Not when the chip is green. Not when you are bored of small dollars.

  • Weeks of clean fills (stops exist, no naked paper, no mid-day rule rewrites).
  • Net that is not one ticket.
  • You can describe the loss you will take next week without flinching.
Yield: if you cannot name the next acceptable losing week in dollars, you are not ready to size up.

06 · Concentration

Example · not a real month

One ticket carrying the scoreboard

+$180hero ticket
−$160rest of the month
Concentration, not edge.

If one ticket is larger than the rest of the month, you do not have a system. You have a lottery hit. Issue 00 already said it. This issue is the reminder when the site shows 100%.

07 · Public logs

How to watch without getting recruited

  • Week table over hero chip.
  • Disaster count over win rate.
  • Hold time: minutes, not "conviction."
  • Delayed tape is a log. It is not a signal booth.

Tips, magazines, and a 100% day do not add up to "this works." They add up to "this account is showing its work."

08 · Killing a book

The honest stop for the experiment

Small experiment money can die. That is the point of calling it an experiment. What should die first is denial.

  • Process disasters (no stop, override a pass, chase) reset the clock. Green weeks do not erase them.
  • A month of no edge and clean process is data. Close it. Do not "one more week" forever.
  • Switching tenor because 0DTE is hard is a new experiment, not a save.

09 · Carry this

Three numbers, every week

  • Fills and record (not just win rate).
  • Net dollars, with and without the best ticket.
  • Disaster count (quote blowups, naked paper, rule rewrites).

If those three are honest, the chip can say anything. Issue 00 is the language. This issue is the scoreboard. Not advice. Not a promise that more sample becomes an edge.

Glossary · Issue 04

Starred terms, in order

n
How many tickets in the sample. n=1 is an anecdote.
Expectancy
Average result per ticket over the sample (wins and losses). Not win rate alone.
Scratch
Small loss or tiny win that is basically "tax + no follow-through."
Concentration
One trade carrying the scoreboard.
