02 · First minute is the tell

Example · not a live ticket

The fuse that never goes green

Never green after entry, on a liquid contract, is usually late — not unlucky side.

The stock can still be "in play." You bought the tail. A quick spike through +15% then a straight line to the stop is often quote + no lock, not "the idea was garbage."

Stock finishes green, option finishes red: you learned issue 03. Do not "prove it" by holding to expiration.

03 · Clean vs sloppy

Grade the process

Clean loser: tight market, fill near the quote you approved, stop or time cut as planned, dollars inside the cap. Tuition. Keep the rulebook.

Sloppy loser: wide open, chase, no stop, extra size, "it should come back," held into the last hour because the day was already red.

Yield: process-clean and $-red can coexist. Process-sloppy and $-green is the dangerous one.

04 · Do not average the autopsy

One hero winner is not the system

One hero winner plus four sloppy losers is not "the system works if you ignore the noise." The hero is the noise if n is small.

Write one line per ticket: late / thin / thesis / clock / exit. If the same word shows up four times in a week, that word is the work — not a new indicator.

05 · What a loser is not

Guardrails

  • Not proof the opposite side would have paid (hindsight).
  • Not proof you should loosen the next entry.
  • Not proof you should skip the next valid tape.
  • One red 0DTE is a sample of one.

Issue 01 is the instrument, issue 03 is the two clocks. This issue is the autopsy. Not advice.

Glossary · Issue 04

Starred terms, in order

Thesis
The specific claim ("this push continues / this push is spent"). Not "I think the stock is good."
Late
Confirmation and fill happened after the burst that would have paid.
Autopsy
Review after the close, with fills and minutes, not mid-session rule rewriting.
