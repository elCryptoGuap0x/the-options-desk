How to read this desk

This issue is a tour of the page, not an options class. It tells you what each piece of the site means so a real ticket, a quiet week, and a "no tickets today" caption all read the same way: honestly.

Job: the site, not the strategy. Options mechanics live in issues 01+. Not advice.

01 · The header

Wireframe · not live data

Clock, state, next open

MARKET OPENNext open: Mon 8:30 CT
The pill tells you if there's a live session right now. If it isn't OPEN, nothing on the page is happening live.

The caption line under the pill is the plain-English recap: what the desk did today (or didn't), in one sentence. No jargon, no setup name.

02 · Week path

Wireframe · not live data

The line under the clock

This week's running result, day by day — not a single ticket's chart.

Up-and-to-the-right for a week is a real week. A flat stretch just means no tickets landed those days. The line does not predict where it goes next.

04 · The dots

Example · not a live ticket

Green = that minute's mark closed above entry. Red = below. Real per-minute data, not a smoothed line.

Tap ▶ Replay to watch the dots reveal in order instead of all at once. It's the same numbers either way — replay is just pacing, not new information.

05 · The delay

Why nothing here is instant

Board and desk activity are shown roughly 15–40 minutes behind real time.

This is deliberate, not laggy infrastructure. A public page that showed a live "about to enter" signal would function like a tip service, which this isn't. Closed tickets post as soon as they're closed — the delay only applies to what's being looked at, not what already happened.
